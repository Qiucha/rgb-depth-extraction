# High-Trust Reference Resources

## Primary Literature & Benchmarks
- **Middlebury Stereo Vision Benchmark (Scharstein & Szeliski)**: [Middlebury Stereo Datasets](pt.technion.ac.il/middlebury) — Standardized benchmark for evaluating stereo matching algorithms against high-precision structured light ground truth.
- **OpenCV Stereo Block Matching & WLS Filtering**: [OpenCV StereoBM Documentation](https://docs.opencv.org/master/dd/530/classcv_1_1StereoBM.html) — Standard implementation guidelines for disparity computation.
- **Structured Light vs. Passive Stereo Sensing**: [Szeliski's Computer Vision: Algorithms and Applications](https://szeliski.org/Book/) — Chapter 11: Dense Motion and Stereo Estimation.
